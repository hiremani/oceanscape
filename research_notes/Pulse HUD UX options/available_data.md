# Available data in the Pulse HUD setup: what is accessible, decoded, and shown

Scope: the oceanscape checkout (`/Users/hiremanirmss/code/projects/fisker/oceanscape`, branch `pulse-coexist`) and the sunnypilot bridge (`/Users/hiremanirmss/code/projects/fisker/openpilot/openpilot/sunnypilot/webhud`). Read-only; nothing was run against a device.

How to read the status columns used below:
- **Received**: the app's CAN helper lets the ID through (`RX` in `tools/gen_ibus.py:52-66`, mirrored in `CanIds.kt` and `ibus_tables.js RX_IDS`). IDs not in `RX` never reach the page even though they are on the bus.
- **Decoded**: a decoder turns the frame into named values. Two decoders exist: the main-thread read-out (`static/js/carstate.js`, every `RX_MESSAGES` signal, generic) and the worker's `FiskerWorld` (`static/js/world/fisker_world.js`, the ADAS mirror plus a few body messages, into a semantic snapshot).
- **Shown**: a UI element reads it. Determined by grepping the main-thread modules for the signal name or snapshot key. Caveat: the *Signals* tab (`static/js/settings.js:552` `renderSignals`) can display any received message raw, so "not shown" means "no purpose-built element", not "invisible to a developer".
- Update rates are the matrix cycle times carried as `cycleMs` in `ibus_tables.js` (0 = event-driven "NoMsgSendType"). Pulse drops about half of IBUS1 and two thirds of IBUS2 frames (`android/README.md:151-154`), so effective rates are lower.

Sources are cited as `path:line` in this checkout; URLs are not applicable.

## KQ1. What do the IBUS tables and `carstate.js` decode, and which decoded signals never reach a UI element?

### Takeaway
The app receives 114 of the 265 messages in the IBUS DBC (38 on IBUS1, 76 on IBUS2) and the generic read-out decodes 703 signals from them, but only 167 signal names are referenced by any main-thread module; the rest (about 536) are decoded and discarded, except where the worker's `FiskerWorld` picks them up. Several high-value domains (tire pressure, 12 V battery, cell temperatures, cabin temperature, heated steering wheel, EPB, trailer, VIN, clock, steering-wheel buttons, range/consumption history) exist on the bus but are not in the receive list at all.

### Inferences
- The largest untapped, already-received groups are the VCU's range/consumption block (`VCU_0x503..0x505`: remaining range `VCU_DrvgMilg`, instant/accumulated consumption, power-limit percentages), the trip counters (`VCU_0x5EA`), the odometer (`BCM_0x641`), the charging fault/reason block (`VCU_0x363`), the BMS measured power/energy (`BMS_0x2F4`), road grade (`ESP_RoadSlop`) and the rear-row belt/occupancy states (`BCM_0x364`). - Adding an ID to `RX` requires re-running `tools/gen_ibus.py` with the matrices (not in the repo) and may change `HW_FILTERS`, which must still pass `DRIVER_DEFAULTS` (`gen_ibus.py:89-92`, `CLAUDE.md` constraints). ### Gaps
- Whether the gateway actually broadcasts each "not received" message on the given bus at the stated cycle could not be verified without a bus capture; the DBC's `CM_ BO_` comments are the matrix's claim.
- Rate figures are matrix cycle times; real arrival rates on Pulse are lower by the documented frame loss and are not measured per message here.

## KQ2. The ADAS mirror (IBUS2) and the comma's ADAS bus: what `fisker_world.js`, `fisker_radar.js` and `adasis.js` decode, and what the UI draws

### Takeaway
`FiskerWorld` builds a rich semantic snapshot (16 groups) from the ADAS messages, but the main thread reads only parts of `vehicle`, `acc`, `assist`, `lanes`, `road`, `objects`, `threats`, `aeb`, `tsr`, `tlr`, `parking`, `warnings` and `power`; the `dms` (driver monitoring), `camera` and ADASIS `horizon` groups have no reader at all, and many sub-fields of the read groups (ISA, DOW, APA state, curbs, TSR confidence/condition, traffic-light lamp count/orientation, lane-change trajectory, AHBA) are decoded and dropped.

### Cited Findings

**Pipeline**
- IBUS2 carries the gateway's mirror of the ADAS bus with identical layout (`INTERNALS.md:176-181`); the page's worker decodes the mirror with the IBUS DBC and, with the comma, the bridge's ADAS frames with `dbc/fisker_ocean_adas_world.dbc` (`CLAUDE.md`, `INTERNALS.md:213-223`). `merge.js:19,97-100` drops the IBUS2 copy of a message for 0.5 s after the comma gave it, so each message has one source.
- ADAS messages the comma streams that the IBUS2 mirror does **not** carry (in `fisker_ocean_adas_world.dbc` but not in `RX` nor in the IBUS DBC): `GW_Syn_All` 0x20, `VCU_0x102` (driver torque request per axle, needed for `power.demandKw`), `ESP_0x116` (rear wheel speeds), `ESP_0x120`, `ADAS_0x121`, `ACU_0x159`, `EPS_0x1C4` (driver steering torque, `EPS_DrvrSteerTq`, used by opendbc), `ADAS_0x1D0`, `VCU_0x214` (gear/ready/pedal on the ADAS bus), `VCU_0x219`, `ADAS_0x481/0x482`, `FCM_0x487`, `ADAS_0x659/0x67E/0x680/0x687/0x689/0x68A` (computed from the two DBCs' `BO_` lines). `ICC_0x531` and `MFS_0x514` exist in the IBUS DBC but are not in `RX`, so on IBUS they are not received.

**Snapshot groups (`fisker_world.js`) and their readers**

- Driver monitoring from the car's own camera (`0x527`: drowsiness level, attention zone, impairment, engagement, camera blocked) is the most complete decoded-but-unused group; it needs no bus or decoder change, only a renderer.

It is a ready cross-check for the OSM road name/speed limit and could fall back when tiles are missing, as `adasis.js:10-12` intends.
- ISA/TSR extras (`isaLimitKph`, `overspeed`, `condition`, `confidence`, `addOn`) would let the speed-limit sign show supplementary plates and a confidence state without new decoding.

## KQ4. What map data is fetched, and which attributes are unused by the UI?

### Takeaway
The road tiles give each way its name, ref, class, lane count, one-way flag, max speed (plus forward/backward and advisory speeds and a hazard text); Overpass adds point features (signals, stop/give-way, crossings, level crossings, mini-roundabouts, traffic calming) and way detail (lanes per direction, turn lanes, width, roundabout, overtaking, lane markings). The UI draws geometry, lane structure, junction arms, point features and the road name, and uses `maxSpeed` only as a bend-speed heuristic; it never shows the map's speed limit, advisory speed, hazard, turn-lane arrows as guidance, overtaking rules or lane-marking absence as information.

### Cited Findings
- Tile decoding (`static/js/world/osmtile.js:131-154`): per way `id`, `name`, `ref`, `cls`/`className` (14 highway classes, `:13-14`), `lanes`, `oneWay`, `maxSpeed`, `maxSpeedForward`, `maxSpeedBackward`, `advisorySpeed`, `hazard` (text), bounds, node coordinates; speeds in m/s. Source: mapd's packed Cap'n Proto tiles from `map-data.pfeifer.dev`, served by `MapTiles.kt`/`maptiles.py` (`INTERNALS.md:236-241`).
- Match output exposes `way{id, name, ref, cls, className, lanes, oneWay, maxSpeed, maxSpeedForward, maxSpeedBackward}` (`mapmatch.js:278-280`) and branches with `name`/`ref`/`layout` (`mapmatch.js:328, 447`). `hud.js:149-171` shows the way's name and ref at the bottom center. `mapsurface.js:750-754` uses `maxSpeed` (else a class default) only to decide which bends are too tight to draw straight. No module reads `advisorySpeed`, `hazard`, `maxSpeedForward/Backward` (grep over `static/js`), and `state.js:382` states explicitly: "no speed limit signs from the map: the camera's read of the real sign is the one shown" (`INTERNALS.md:297-298` gives the reason: map limits are often stale).
- The roads layer (`state.js:285-323`) carries per way `maxSpeed`, `turnsFwd`/`turnsBwd`, `passFwd`/`passBwd`, `markings`, `singleTrack`, `width`, `laneW`, `fwd`/`bwd`/`centre` lane counts. `roadnet.js`/`roadnetworker.js` draw strips, edges, centerlines and lane boundaries (`INTERNALS.md:274-282`); turn arrows and overtaking flags are present in the layer but their drawing was not located in the grep (`turnsFwd` appears only in `state.js` and `mapmatch.js` layouts), so treat them as "available in the layer, drawing unverified".
- Way detail from Overpass (`MapFeatures.kt:206-212` `WAYS_QUERY`, `WAY_TAGS`; parsed by `roaddetail.js:55-78`): `width`, `est_width`, `lanes`, `lanes:forward/backward/both_ways`, `turn:lanes(:forward/backward)`, `junction` (roundabout), `overtaking(:forward/backward)`, `divider`, `lane_markings`. One request per 0.25 deg tile, kept as `ways3_<tlat>_<tlon>.json`.
- Point features (`MapFeatures.kt:197-205` `QUERY`, `KEEP_TAGS`): nodes with `highway` in {traffic_signals, stop, give_way, crossing, mini_roundabout}, `railway=level_crossing`, `traffic_calming`; tags kept: `direction`, `traffic_signals:direction`, `traffic_signals`, `crossing`, `crossing:markings`, `stop`. The worker keeps those on a known road's node (`state.js:328-385`), with arrival directions; `mapfeatures.js` draws heads, signs, stripes, crossbucks (`INTERNALS.md:285-297`). The Overpass `traffic_signals` tag value (e.g. pedestrian vs signal type) is kept but not used in `state.js` (only `crossing`, `crossing:markings`, `stop`, `direction` are read at `:352-359, 378-379`).
- Prefetch: `POST /map/prefetch {lat, lon, radius_km}` every 3 km or 2 min, default 25 km (`main.js:30` `mapPrefetchKm`, `INTERNALS.md:302-308`); `GET /map/status` progress is shown in settings.
- Not fetched at all: OSM speed-limit signs, place names, POIs, building footprints, elevation, lane widths per lane, bus lanes, `maxspeed:conditional`, `surface`, `lit` (no query asks for them; `QUERY` and `WAYS_QUERY` are the only Overpass queries in the app, and `maptiles.py` on the comma mirrors them per `INTERNALS.md:286-288`).

### Inferences
- A map speed-limit readout (with "map" provenance and `speedLimitAhead` from mapd) is possible today from `way.maxSpeed`; the authors deliberately chose the camera's sign. Showing it as a secondary, differently styled value would respect that choice.
- Turn-lane arrows from `turn:lanes` are already parsed and aligned to the drawn lanes (`roaddetail.js:92-114`); if not yet painted on the lane surface, that is a renderer addition, not a data one.
- `hazard` and `advisorySpeed` from the tiles are free signals for curve warnings.

### Gaps
- I did not read `roadnet.js`/`roadnetgeom.js`/`mapsurface.js` in full to confirm whether turn arrows or the `markings=false` flag are rendered; the grep hits suggest they are carried but their drawing was not found.
- Overpass availability and rate limits on the car's network are operational matters not assessable here.

## KQ5. What does the Android layer provide that the page does not yet surface?

### Cited Findings

Lane guidance, full route, ETA as a time (only the text's "ETA" is passed through) are not available from notifications.
- CAN state snapshot and diagnostics: `canDiag()` output is shown in the Signals tab's link check (`MainActivity.kt:143-147`); the helper's `tx ... ok/error` counts are in `CanBridge.txReport()` (`CanBridge.kt:57-60`).
- Local server (`LocalServer.kt:119-131`): relays `/ws`, `/video`, `/api/*` to the comma; serves `/map/tile`, `/map/features`, `/map/ways`, `/map/prefetch`, `/map/status`; `/app/comma/*` (`CommaManager.kt:47`: key, SSH, jobs start/stop/restart/reboot/fetch/pull/branches/branch/install/restore/panda_build/exec per `assets/comma/actions.sh:21-80`, `info.py`, `routes.py`) and `/app/update/*` (`AppUpdater.kt:58`; GitHub latest release, SHA-256 and signer checks, `android/README.md:199-209`). Jobs and installs are refused while the car moves (`android/README.md:170-173, 209`).
- Theme: the page tells the app its theme and the app follows the car's day/night mode for *Auto* (`android/README.md:56-57, 68-71`); the car's own `BCM_VehAmbBri` and `BCM_ValueLightFrnt_RLS` are received but not used for theming.
- Foreground service keeps the CAN reader, link and server alive off screen (`android/README.md:12-14`, `HudService.kt`).

### Inferences
- The Android side already has what a "position quality" chip would need for the device source (accuracy, provider, age), and the TBOX quality messages cover the car source (KQ1); neither is surfaced.
- The notification route for navigation cannot provide lane guidance or a route polyline; any richer nav UI would need the ADASIS horizon (KQ2) or a different nav source.
- Root-only features (ARP discovery, hardware filters, notification access, silent install) degrade gracefully per the docs; a UI indicator of "running without root" would make the missing read-outs (`INTERNALS.md:198-203`) explainable.

### Gaps
- `HudListener.kt` and `infotainment.js` were only grepped, not read fully; the exact set of media fields rendered (e.g. the source app name) is inferred from element names.
- Which app will emit navigation notifications on the car's head unit is documented as unknown (`android/README.md:97-100`).

## KQ6. Which signals are flagged as unverified or wrong?

### Takeaway
The authors document a small set of matrix errors and calibrations: ADAS object distances at 0.2 m/bit (not 0.5), a range/lateral/latency calibration applied in the world model, the 0x255 ADASIS header bit offset, the external-discharge counter mislabel, the lane curvature/heading sign conventions exposed as toggles, and a draft radar DBC with several scales still open; several decoded fields are explicitly "unknown meaning".

### Cited Findings
- `CORRECTIONS` (`tools/gen_world_dbc.py:37-47`): `ADAS_ObjN_LongDist/LatDist` are 0.2 m/bit "measured on the car; the matrix says 0.5" (checked against openpilot's lead at ~42 m); paired with the radar the range fits ~0.25 m/bit from about the rear axle and the lateral reads ~1.35x wide, corrected in `world_model.MEASURED_CALIBRATION` "until a tape-measured check settles them". `gen_ibus.py:36` imports the same corrections into the IBUS tables.
- `MEASURED_CALIBRATION` (`INTERNALS.md:115-127`): ADAS range `x / 0.80 - 3.7`, lateral 1.35x, latency ~0.23 s; radar Doppler 1/16 m/s per bit (not 0.06) and 0.6 deg left; wheel speed 3.1% under GPS; openpilot leads 0.2 m short. Switchable under *Display → Geometry calibration*.
- `LSB_CORRECTIONS` (`gen_ibus.py:38-42`): the IBUS2 matrix has `ICC_0x255`'s ADASIS header three bits off; corrected to the standard layout the car sends. `INTERNALS.md:349` repeats it.
- `adasis.js:22-30`: the curvature profile conversion is a fit against yaw rate/speed from two drives ("the spec's own table should replace this once at hand"); `INTERNALS.md:70-72`: the sign-type table of `ICC_0x252` is not in the matrix, so signs are unused.
- `energy.js:5-6`: the matrix mislabels the external-discharge counter as battery thermal management; signal names taken as truth. `energy.js:90-98, 213-216`: this car's VCU leaves totals and percentage shares at zero.
- `INTERNALS.md:351-353`: the sign of lane curvature and of object heading are not documented in the matrix; exposed as toggles, verified on the car for lane heading only.
- `fisker_world.js:28-31`: lane heading convention (90 deg = parallel, grows to the right) "checked against the road on the car"; `:343`: `ADAS_HzdDetected` 0 means TRUE "in this DBC"; `:471-474`: `ADAS_TrafficLightShape` "despite its name carries Red/Amber/Green"; `:244`: ACC set speed is in the cluster unit.
- `fisker_radar.js:25-26`: radar class values are "tentative" (0 unclassified, 1 car, 6 pedestrian, 8 small). Radar DBC (`dbc/fisker_ocean_mrr.dbc:1295-1354` comments): draft from one 61 s parking-lot drive; `DistLat` scale "matches but only to ~10%"; `ArelLong` "0.03 vs 1/32 not resolved"; `*_Std` scale unknown; `Quality` "meaning unknown"; `DistCorr` "probably a correlation term"; clock message upper bits "probably the date but did not decode"; several constant/unknown bytes. No elevation is decoded, so overhead structures read as stopped cars (`INTERNALS.md:134-137`).
- `INTERNALS.md:348`: the TBOX IMU angular rates "don't match the chassis gyro's scale and aren't used".
- Fix quality gating: `state.js:173` accepts a fix only when `TBOX_GPSFixOK == 1` or the message is absent (comma buses), and `TBOX_HorzAccuracy > 0`.
- `android/README.md:247-249`: the UK traffic-side and camera sign-unit overrides have JS tests but "car hardware validation is still required".
- Frame loss: Pulse drops about half of IBUS1 and two thirds of IBUS2 frames (`android/README.md:151-154`), so status read-outs settle but lag.

### Inferences
- Any UX built on ADAS object positions should present them as calibrated estimates; the two calibration settings (0.2 m/bit decode and the world-model correction) interact, and the raw view differs from the fused one (`INTERNALS.md:341`).
- Radar-derived UI should remain opt-in/debug until the open scales are closed, as the authors keep it (default *Radar objects* on only for checking decoding per `INTERNALS.md:90-93`; `main.js:27` sets `showRadar: true`).

### Gaps
- No document lists which IBUS matrix signals (beyond the ones above) have been verified on the car; the "From the car" controls are implicitly verified by use, the rest of the 703 decoded signals are matrix-only.

## KQ7. Write/command capabilities and their constraints

### Takeaway
The app may send only seven IBUS1 message IDs (`0x4E, 0x530, 0x534, 0x90, 0x528, 0x533, 0x52`), all head-unit control messages, enforced in `CanBridge.send` and in the Go helper's per-interface allowlist, rate-limited to 60 frames/s, refused while the bus is silent; cyclic head-unit state messages (drive mode/charging, units/brightness, ambient/California mode) and the audio messages are deliberately excluded, and nothing may be sent on IBUS2 or to the comma's buses. Comma management jobs, CAN overrides, SSH key changes, replay and app installs are refused while the car moves.

### Cited Findings
- Allowlist: `TX = {"IBUS1": [0x4E, 0x530, 0x534, 0x90, 0x528, 0x533, 0x52], "IBUS2": []}` (`gen_ibus.py:74-77`), generated into `CanIds.TX` and `ibus_tables.js TX_IDS`. Rationale (`gen_ibus.py:67-73`): these are sent only on driver touch (OnWriteWithRepetition) or, for `0x528/0x533/0x52`, cyclically with "no request" idle values. Never `0x610` (drive mode, charging), `0x529` (units, brightness), `0x336` (ambient, California Mode; carries an E2E counter), `0x46`/`0x44` (one frame carries every audio volume, mute and EQ with no idle value and no feedback to copy).
- Enforcement: `CanBridge.send` (`CanBridge.kt:114-139`) rejects an ID not in `CanIds.TX[bus]`, a bad length, a silent bus (`awake(bus)`), and more than `TX_PER_SECOND` frames per second (comment at `:23`; the check at `:123-125`); repeats are clamped to 1..10 at 5..500 ms gaps (`:120, :136`); every send is logged (`:129-130`). The helper opens a transmit socket only for the IDs passed as `tx:canN=...` (`main.go:6-12, 126-137, 220-235`) and answers `error not allowed` otherwise (`:271-272`). What the app sends never loops back into what it reads (`android/README.md:120-121`).
- What the controls send (`carcatalog.js` `MSG` map `:33`, request calls): `0x4E` `ICC_LoBeamCtrl`, `ICC_CentrLockCtrl`, `ICC_MirrCmd`, `ICC_ReMirrAutoFoldSet`, `ICC_SunroofPercCtrlCmdReq`, `ICC_ReDefrstOpenReq`, `ICC_abdEnaReq`, `ICC_ArmedClsdWinSet`, `ICC_DoorUnlockSet`, `ICC_OffUnlckSet`, `ICC_RainClsdSunroofSet` (the window `ICC_*WinCtrl`, lamp, wiper/washer, fog, sunshade and trunk-lock signals exist in the same message per `ibus_tables.js TX_MESSAGES`; `INTERNALS.md:189-191` lists windows auto up/down, liftgate, lamps as sent); `0x530` `ICC_ACSwtReq`, `ICC_AirClnSwtReq`, `ICC_AirVolSet`, `ICC_ECCAUTOReq`, `ICC_ECCIntExtCircReq`, `ICC_MaxFrntDefrstSet` (also carries `ICC_DrvrTSet`/`ICC_PassTSet` temperatures and `ICC_NavSpdLim*`/`ICC_NavRoadTyp`/`ICC_NavCtryCod` navigation speed-limit fields); `0x534` `ICC_ECCSysSwtCmd`, `ICC_DrvrBlowModReq`, `ICC_BackRowAirOutlModReq`; `0x52` `ICC_TrActnCmd` (liftgate; also `ICC_SetMaxPosnCmd_PLGM`); `0x90` `ICC_AutoLockUnlockCmd` (also `ICC_SeatLocnMemOperCmd`); `0x528` seat heat requests, `ICC_SWH_Req` (steering wheel heat), `ICC_SeatWelFctEnaReq`, `ICC_DrvrID`; `0x533` seat moves and lumbar (per `TX_MESSAGES`). The grep of request calls may miss helpers built with `req()`/`press()` wrappers, so the per-message signal lists from `ibus_tables.js` are the authoritative bound.
- Disabled reasons in the catalog (`carcatalog.js:49-52`, counts from grep): `OWNED` (20 controls: "the head unit sends this setting itself every second"), `NO_MSG` (10), `MOCK` (14, nothing on the car), `AUDIO_FRAME` (2), `ADAS` (37: driver-assistance settings are set under *CAN settings*, which the comma sends as the `ICC_0x52A` override; on this branch `/api/overrides` is unsupported, `sunnypilot/webhud/README.md` first paragraph). A control that cannot send stays greyed with its reason, not hidden (`CLAUDE.md`).
- The comma path: CAN overrides, SSH keys and replay are refused while the car moves, and writes are accepted from private addresses only, never cross-origin (`sunnypilot/webhud/README.md`, "Writes are accepted..."); the app's comma jobs are refused while the car moves using the bridge's `/api/status` or the speed the page sends (`android/README.md:170-173`); app install is refused while moving (`android/README.md:209`).
- Hardware filters must keep everything Pulse's own app expects (`DRIVER_DEFAULTS`, `gen_ibus.py:81-92`; `CLAUDE.md` "do not narrow it").
- The head unit's navigation speed-limit fields in `0x530` (`ICC_NavSpdLim`, `ICC_NavSpdLimUnit`, `ICC_NavSpdLimSts`, `ICC_NavRoadTyp`, `ICC_NavCtryCod`, `ICC_NavSpdLimExitDst`) are inside an allowed message; whether sending them is safe or meaningful is not discussed in the docs (the message is event-driven, "NoMsgSendType", `ibus_tables.js` `cycle=0`).

### Inferences
- UX enhancements that act on the car are bounded to body/climate/seat/liftgate/lock-setting requests already modelled; anything touching drive mode, regen, charge limits, units, brightness, ambient light or audio is out of scope by design and documented as such.
- Because the allowed messages carry more signals than the catalog uses (windows, wipers, fog, sunshade, seat memory, steering-wheel heat), new controls within those messages need only catalog entries, but each must respect the message's idle values (`init` in `ibus_tables.js`) and the "request then idle" behaviour (`ibus_tables.js` header comment).

### Gaps
- Whether `ICC_0x530`'s navigation speed-limit signals influence the ADAS ISA/TSR display on the car is not documented anywhere in these repos.
- `cancmd.js`'s own moving guard, if any, was not confirmed (grep found the guard text only in `carcontrols.js:832` for ACC settings); `CanBridge.send` itself does not check speed, only bus liveness and rate.
